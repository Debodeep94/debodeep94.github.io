About me

I am a PhD researcher in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Trento and the University of Pisa. I work at the intersection of human-centered AI, machine learning, and decision making.

My research focuses on hybrid human–machine learning: how humans and AI can collaborate in learning, reasoning, and decision processes. I am interested in intelligent systems that combine algorithmic strength with human judgment, and in guidance that supports people in making decisions.

Background

Before my PhD, I completed a Master’s degree in Data Science at Sapienza University of Rome and an MSc in Statistics at the University of Madras. This path from statistics to AI has shaped how I approach questions about learning and decision making.

I previously worked as an AI intern at Baker Hughes (Nuovo Pignone Tecnologie), and as an extracurricular intern at Project Consulting SRL, working on AI applications in video data.

Originally from Kolkata, India, I am now based in Italy. Outside academia, I enjoy literature, travelling, chess, and writing poems and short stories.
